Xcel Support

Xcel is an iOS app by Xtinct AI that turns your week into a 7-game playoff series - journal your day, get an AI Win/Loss verdict, and chase the comeback.

Get in touch

The fastest way to reach us is the in-app feedback screen: open Xcel → Home → "Send feedback." Pick a category (Bug, UI/UX, Feature idea, AI judge, or Other) and send it straight to us.

You can also email us directly at xtinctai@outlook.com.

Common questions

  • Why was my day marked a Loss? The AI judge reviews your checklist and evening notes against what you planned that morning. If you think a call was wrong, use a Challenge Call (one per series) to make your case.
  • I missed logging a day - can I fix it? No - a missed day is an automatic Loss by design, and days lock once judged. This is intentional; see the Challenge Call feature if you believe it shouldn't count.
  • Does my data leave my phone? No, except feedback you choose to send. See the Privacy Policy for details.
