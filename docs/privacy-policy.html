Xcel Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Xcel ("the app") is built by Xtinct AI. This page explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and what it never does.

Your journal stays on your device

Everything you log in Xcel — your daily entries, checklists, photos, AI verdicts, and stats — is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). None of it is uploaded to us or to any third party. There is no account, no server, and no cloud sync.

AI judging happens on-device

The daily Win/Loss verdict is generated on-device using Apple's Apple Intelligence (FoundationModels) framework. Your entry text is never sent to Xtinct AI, Apple's servers, or any other party for this purpose.

Permissions the app requests

  • Camera — to snap same-day photo proof of a completed task. Photos are analyzed on-device and never leave your phone.
  • Photo Library — to attach an existing photo as proof of a task. Checked on-device (creation date + on-device image recognition) and never leaves your phone.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition — to let you dictate your evening log instead of typing. Transcription happens on-device; audio is not recorded or transmitted anywhere.
  • Notifications — to remind you to set your morning intention and log your evening result.

Feedback you choose to send

If you use the in-app "Send feedback" screen, the text you write — along with your app version, build number, iOS version, and device model — is sent as an email to xtinctai@outlook.com through your device's own Mail app. This is the only data that ever leaves your device, and only when you choose to send it.

No analytics, no ads, no tracking

Xcel does not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking of any kind. We do not sell or share your data because we don't have it.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to xtinctai@outlook.com.
